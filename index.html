M.Sc. thesis, 2026

LLM-guided Socratic hint generation with student-state awareness

Research code for my thesis. A Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct model, fine-tuned with QLoRA on MathDial tutoring dialogues on a 6 GB laptop GPU, estimates the student's mastery from the dialogue and then writes the next Socratic hint conditioned on that estimate. Across 241 held-out dialogues and five conditions, fine-tuning improved LLM-judged hint quality over the same base model without fine-tuning (all p < 0.01). State-conditioning produced no significant gain, and a small first-run deficit did not replicate over three seeds. A same-history counterfactual with a placebo check showed that most of the model's response to an injected state came from the wording of the state line. The prompted Claude Sonnet 5 baseline still leads on every measure.

Deutsche Bahn punctuality: where trains lose time

Follows ICE, IC and EC journeys stop by stop (491,562 stop events, June and August 2026) and splits each train's delay into time lost while running and time lost while standing at stations. Trains spend 8% of their scheduled time at stations but add 52% of their net delay there (51% in June). Built as a tested pipeline: Python ingestion, DuckDB, dbt models with 45 data tests, a Streamlit dashboard and a monthly refresh in GitHub Actions.

Causal inference

LaLonde causal benchmark

Rebuilds LaLonde's 1986 exercise: keeps the 185 treated participants of the NSW job-training experiment, swaps the randomized controls for 15,992 people from the CPS, and scores five observational estimators against the experimental benchmark of +$1,794. The naive difference in means flips sign (−$8,498), and only OLS regression adjustment lands inside the benchmark's confidence interval. Overlap and balance diagnostics show why the weighting estimators (IPW, AIPW) break down.

Heterogeneous treatment effects (CATE)

Compares S-, T-, X-, and DR-learners on how well they recover the conditional average treatment effect on synthetic data where the true effect is known, under a confounded and a randomized scenario, with 5-fold cross-fitting for all nuisance models. The X-learner has the lowest PEHE under confounding (0.193), the S-learner under randomization (0.158). When targeting the top 5% of units, the X- and DR-learners come within about 3% of an oracle ranking.

Minimum wage: staggered difference-in-differences

Do state minimum wage increases raise unemployment? Joins U.S. Department of Labor minimum wage records with FRED state unemployment rates (1976–2019) and runs both naive two-way fixed effects and the Callaway–Sant'Anna estimator for staggered adoption. The two estimates land within about 15% of each other and neither is significant at the 5% level. The event study shows no pre-trend and an association that builds slowly over several years after treatment.

Aspect sentiment analysis for food reviews

Compares fine-tuned BERT, RoBERTa, and XLM-RoBERTa with LSTM and TF-IDF + SVM baselines on 1.31M Zomato review instances. Of the test reviews, 97.5% were exact duplicates of training text, which inflated the baselines' accuracy. On the 4,947 reviews never seen in training, RoBERTa did best (83.0%). Per-aspect sentiment is evaluated with distant supervision on single-aspect reviews, and the model is served through a tested FastAPI service.

Telco churn risk API

A tuned, calibrated churn classifier served through FastAPI, with a /predict endpoint and an /explain endpoint that uses SHAP to show why a customer was flagged. Logistic regression beat random forest and gradient boosting in 5-fold cross-validation (ROC-AUC 0.845). The decision threshold (0.10) is chosen from assumed costs of $50 per unnecessary retention offer and $500 per missed churner, rather than the default of 0.5. Packaged with Docker and tested in CI.

CAISO electricity demand forecast

Day-ahead forecast of CAISO hourly electricity demand on data pulled live from the EIA and Open-Meteo APIs. A walk-forward backtest over 61 windows compares a seasonal-naive baseline, Prophet, and gradient boosting. Gradient boosting cuts mean MAPE to 2.89% against the baseline's 8.04%, while Prophet does not beat the naive baseline on this data.

Cookie Cats A/B test

Analysis of a public A/B test, run on about 90,000 players, that moved a mobile game's first gate from level 30 to level 40. Covers a sample-ratio check, power analysis, two-proportion z-tests with bootstrap confidence intervals, and Benjamini–Hochberg correction across metrics. Day-7 retention is significantly higher with the gate at level 30. A peeking simulation shows that stopping early pushes the false-positive rate to 20%.
